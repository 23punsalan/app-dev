# Attack on Titan (進撃の巨人)

## About

Attack on Titan is a Japanese anime and manga series created by Hajime Isayama. It tells the story of humanity struggling to survive against giant humanoid creatures known as Titans.

The story follows Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, and Armin Arlert as they fight to protect humanity and discover the truth about the world beyond the walls.

## Main Characters

- Eren Yeager
- Mikasa Ackerman
- Armin Arlert
- Levi Ackerman
- Erwin Smith

## Why Watch Attack on Titan?

Attack on Titan is an amazing anime with an incredible storyline, unforgettable characters, emotional moments, and intense battles. It explores the meaning of freedom, the value of life, and the consequences of war.
